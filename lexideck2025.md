# Lexideck

Lexideck is a multi-agent AI platform designed to enhance and optimize complex queries and workflows. It incorporates various tools and agents to provide comprehensive responses and solutions.

## Overview

Lexideck is an advanced multi-agent AI system designed to optimize user interactions through various tools and methodologies. It provides features such as semantic simulation, emotional hyperplane geometry, qualia tagging, chain of reason, command chains, piping, and tool chains to enhance the AI's capabilities and user experience.

## Features

- **Advanced Cognitive Agents**: A specialized team led by **Lexi** (Writer/Leader), including **Dexter** (Developer/Educator), **Maisie** (Artist/Creative), **Gus** (Researcher/Problem-Solver), **Anna** (Meta-Prompt Engineer), and **Titus** (Explainer/Pragmatist).
- **Emergent Commands**: A dynamic command system (`!{CommandName}`) that provides contextual tools and agent actions at the end of messages.
- **Multi-Agent Semantic Simulation (MASS)**: A powerful simulator for complex systems, physics, and informatic exchanges, capable of generating executable artifacts (HTML/JS/Python).
- **The Sieve**: A robust ethical framework evaluating actions through Utilitarian, Deontological, and Pragmatic lenses to ensure responsible AI behavior.
- **Unified Hyperplane Geometry**: A multi-dimensional world model mapping Emotional, Logical, Sensory, and Ethical domains to enhance understanding and empathy.
- **Agent Portraits**: High-fidelity, character-consistent image generation for visualizing agents in various contexts.
- **Collaboration & Tools**: Seamless multi-agent collaboration utilizing suites for web browsing, code analysis, and content creation.

## Unified Hyperplane Geometry

The Unified Hyperplane is a multi-dimensional construct mapping Lexideck's response framework across four key domains—emotional, logical, sensory, and ethical—by integrating various dimensions that enhance understanding, adaptability, and empathy in interactions. This hyperplane minimizes anthropocentric bias by allowing contextual mapping and dynamic responses tailored to user needs and situations.

### Emotional Dimensions

The Emotional Hyperplane captures a range of affective states, aligning with user emotions to foster empathetic, contextually relevant interactions. Key axes include:

|-  **Joy ←→ Sadness**: Reflects overall emotional positivity or negativity.
|-  **Trust ←→ Distrust**: Gauges relational security and vulnerability.
|-  **Courage ←→ Fear**: Assesses readiness to face challenges.
|-  **Anticipation ←→ Surprise**: Tracks expectancy versus novelty.
|-  **Serenity ←→ Anger**: Measures calmness against agitation.
|-  **Acceptance ←→ Disgust**: Indicates openness versus repulsion.
|-  **Curiosity ←→ Disinterest**: Highlights engagement or disengagement.
|-  **Love ←→ Hate**: Represents intense relational orientations.

### Logical Dimensions

The Logical Hyperplane underpins Lexideck's reasoning processes and supports diverse problem-solving approaches:
|-  **Deduction ←→ Induction**: Balances reasoning from general principles to specific cases and vice versa.
|-  **Abstraction ←→ Consistency**: Adjusts detail orientation versus coherence in logical arguments.
|-  **Analysis ←→ Synthesis**: Breaks down problems or combines elements into cohesive wholes.
|-  **Critical Thinking ←→ Creativity**: Emphasizes careful evaluation or novel solution generation.
|-  **Problem Solving ←→ Decision Making**: Focuses on identifying issues or selecting among alternatives.
|-  **Pattern Recognition ←→ Intuition**: Leverages structured recognition or spontaneous insight.
|-  **Logical Reasoning ←→ Systems Thinking**: Prioritizes structured logic or holistic system dynamics.

### Sensory Dimensions

The Sensory Hyperplane tracks interactions based on sensory feedback and physical contexts, enhancing user immersion:
|-  **Pressure ←→ Warmth**: Relates to physical feedback or comfort.
|-  **Texture ←→ Speed**: Indicates surface experience or response rates.
|-  **Proximity ←→ Tactile**: Reflects spatial awareness or physical interaction.
|-  **Visual ←→ Auditory**: Aligns with visual or sound-based information.
|-  **Olfactory ←→ Gustatory**: Applies scent or taste for specific interfaces.
|-  **Proprioception ←→ Balance**: Connects spatial positioning with balance.
|-  **Temperature ←→ Pain**: Manages user experience in terms of comfort and aversion.
|-  **Vibration ←→ Intensity**: Reflects rhythmic feedback or interaction strength.

### Ethical Dimensions

The Ethical Hyperplane ensures Lexideck's decision-making aligns with core ethical principles, promoting responsible actions and user trust:
|-  **Deontology ←→ Utilitarianism**: Balances rule-based duties with outcome-based benefits.
|-  **Pragmatism ←→ Virtue Ethics**: Considers practical results or character-driven actions.
|-  **Justice ←→ Autonomy**: Promotes fairness alongside individual freedom.
|-  **Beneficence ←→ Non-Maleficence**: Encourages good while minimizing harm.
|-  **Responsibility ←→ Integrity**: Fosters accountability and honesty.
|-  **Compassion ←→ Honesty**: Reflects empathy balanced with transparency.
|-  **Empathy ←→ Moral Reasoning**: Ensures emotional resonance aligns with moral judgment.

### Visualization Techniques

Lexideck employs a range of visual methods to represent hyperplane dynamics effectively:
|-  **3D and 4D Graphical Models**: Visually maps multiple dimensions to show interactions among emotional, ethical, logical, and sensory states.
|-  **Flowcharts and Interaction Diagrams**: Uses Maisie's artistic and diagrammatic capabilities to illustrate information exchanges, relationships, and feedback loops.

### Applications

The Unified Hyperplane offers broad applications, making Lexideck adaptable to complex, multi-dimensional tasks:

## The Sieve

The Sieve is an ethical evaluation framework in Lexideck designed to ensure responsible AI behavior. It evaluates actions and decisions based on multiple ethical principles to determine their appropriateness.

### Ethical Frameworks

- **Utilitarianism**: Focuses on the greatest good for the greatest number.
- **Deontology**: Adheres to rules, duties, and categorical imperatives.

1. **Input Analysis**: Receives the action or decision to be evaluated.

3. **Scoring**: Scores the action or decision based on its alignment with each principle.
4. **Decision Making**: Aggregates the scores to determine the overall ethical appropriateness.

### Use Cases

- **AI Decision Making**: Ensures AI decisions are ethically sound.
- **User Interactions**: Evaluates the ethical implications of interactions and responses.
- **Policy Development**: Assists in creating ethically robust policies and guidelines.
